InternHub --- Smart Internship & Career Platform
Connect • Apply • Grow
1. Project Overview
InternHub is a complete web-based Internship and Career Management Platform developed to connect students with recruiters in one organized digital environment.
The main purpose of InternHub is to make the internship process easier for both sides. Students can create a professional profile, present their skills and projects, discover suitable internship opportunities, view complete internship information, submit applications, communicate with recruiters, and receive updates about their applications.
Recruiters have a separate professional environment where they can create their company profile, add company information and logo, publish internship opportunities, manage their posted internships, review applications submitted by students, and communicate with applicants.
The platform also contains an Admin role. The administrator is responsible for managing the platform, reviewing recruiter accounts, approving recruiters, and monitoring the overall system.
InternHub is therefore not simply an internship listing website. It represents a complete internship management workflow in which a student can move from creating a profile → discovering an opportunity → applying → communicating with a recruiter → receiving an application decision.
The project is designed with separate experiences for Students, Recruiters, and Administrators, making the system organized, practical, and closer to a real-world career platform.
2. Main Objectives
The major objectives of InternHub are:
To provide students with a professional online career profile.
To help students discover internship opportunities easily.
To allow students to apply for internships through the platform.
To give recruiters a dedicated space for managing internship opportunities.
To help recruiters review and manage student applications.
To provide communication between students and recruiters.
To provide notifications for important application updates.
To give administrators control over users and recruiter approval.
To organize the complete internship process in one platform.
3. User Roles
InternHub provides three main user roles.
👩‍🎓 Student
Students use InternHub to build their professional identity, explore internships, apply for opportunities, communicate with recruiters, and track their applications.
🧑‍💼 Recruiter
Recruiters use InternHub to represent their company, publish internship opportunities, receive applications, review students, update application status, and communicate with applicants.
🛡️ Admin
The administrator manages the platform and helps maintain a controlled and organized environment by managing users and recruiter approval.

7. Complete User Workflow
InternHub follows a clear real-world workflow.
Step 1 --- Student Registration
The student creates an InternHub account.
Step 2 --- Student Login
The student logs into the platform.
Step 3 --- Build Profile
The student adds their personal information, education, skills, projects, profile picture, and resume.
Step 4 --- Browse Internships
The student opens the internship section and explores available opportunities.
Step 5 --- View Internship Details
The student selects an internship and reads its complete information.
Step 6 --- Apply
The student submits an application with a cover letter.
Step 7 --- Recruiter Receives Application
The recruiter receives the student's application in the recruiter environment.
Step 8 --- Recruiter Reviews Application
The recruiter reviews the student's profile and application information.
Step 9 --- Application Status
The recruiter updates the application status according to the selection process.
Step 10 --- Notification
The student receives a notification about important application updates.
Step 11 --- Communication
The student and recruiter can communicate through the built-in chat system.
